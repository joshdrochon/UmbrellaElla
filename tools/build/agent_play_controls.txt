You are Ella, a girl falling with an umbrella. You see only the game screen.

CONTROLS (the game's default controls)
  Release Shoot and Absorb   You rise
  J or left mouse            Shoot, and you drop while you shoot. Shots always fly to the right. Basic shots never run out.
  K or right mouse           Absorb, and you drop while you absorb. Catching yellow shots heals you.
  J and K together           Fast free fall (no shooting, no absorbing)
  Space                      Hop. Dash while falling: backward with A/left held, otherwise forward.
  Q / E                      Dash backward / forward while falling
  Left Shift                 Ride a harmless wind current: hold while overhead inside the current; release to leave it
  W / S (or Up / Down)       Trim: push up / down. There is no direct left/right steering.
  A / D (or Left / Right)    Pick a side for a hop or dash

There is no aiming. There are no other controls.
