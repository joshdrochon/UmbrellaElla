# VISUAL QA path (tools/build/visual_qa.py): one run that captures every screen a player sees, for the Opus judge.
# Title -> Start Game play -> Defeated (assisted hits) -> Quit to Title -> Director's Cut route -> Keeper fight
# (route assisted to the handoff) -> assisted win -> credits. Shot names are the screen ids visual_qa.py reads (vq_<id>).
firstrun
wait Title 60
idle 2
shot vq_title
click Start Game
trywait Tutorial 5
keyuntil Playing Enter 0.8 30
idle 3
hold J 1.0                        # real shooting, so gold shots are on screen
shot vq_play
hits Defeat 1 1.2 90              # assisted: 1 damage per 1.2 s through Ella's Health hit path until Defeated
idle 1
shot vq_defeated
click Quit to Title
wait Title 60
idle 1
click Director's Cut
skipunless Purchase 3             # a run before this one earned points: Purchase opens first; leave it
key Escape
wait Title 5
click Director's Cut
trywait Tutorial 5
keyuntil Playing Enter 0.8 30
idle 2
route 1 30 1                      # real keys toward CP1 (may fail; recorded)
routeassist 1
idle 1
skipunless Purchase 2             # a refuge with unspent points opens Purchase; leave it without spending
key Escape
idle 1
route 2 6 1                       # a few seconds of real flying past the refuge, on the route
shot vq_dc_route
routeassist 4
idle 3
hold J 1.0
shot vq_keeper
assistwin 900
wait Credits 180
idle 3
shot vq_credits
end
