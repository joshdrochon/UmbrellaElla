# VISUAL QA path (tools/build/visual_qa.py): one run that captures every screen a player sees, for the Opus judge.
# Title -> Start Game play -> Defeated (assisted hits) -> Quit to Title -> Director's Cut route -> Keeper fight
# (route assisted to the handoff) -> assisted win -> credits. Shot names are the screen ids visual_qa.py reads (vq_<id>).
firstrun
wait Title 60
idle 2
shot vq_title
click Start Game
trywait Tutorial 5
keyuntil Playing Enter 0.8 30
idle 1                            # short: with nothing held she rises into the lethal ceiling in ~4 s (D-new-ceiling-lethal)
hold J 1.0                        # real shooting, so gold shots are on screen
shot vq_play
figureshot vq_figure              # Ella's normal frame + a plate with her hidden (visual_qa figure_check)
hurtshot vq_hurt                  # one assisted life lost; captured while she shows Hurt (black box / sliver check)
hits Defeat 1 1.2 90              # assisted: 1 damage per 1.2 s through Ella's Health hit path until Defeated
idle 1
shot vq_defeated
click Quit to Title
wait Title 60
idle 1
click Director's Cut
skipunless Purchase 3             # a run before this one earned points: Purchase opens first; leave it
key Escape
wait Title 5
click Director's Cut
trywait Tutorial 2                # short waits: idle rise reaches the lethal ceiling (D-new-ceiling-lethal)
keyuntil Playing Enter 0.8 30
idle 1
route 1 30 1                      # real keys toward CP1 (may fail; recorded)
routeassist 1
idle 1
skipunless Purchase 2             # a refuge with unspent points opens Purchase; leave it without spending
key Escape
idle 1
route 2 6 1                       # a few seconds of real flying past the refuge, on the route
shot vq_dc_route
routeassist 4
idle 3
hold J 1.0
shot vq_keeper
idle 0.8
shot vq_keeper_a1                   # detector-only Keeper shots: attacks on screen in some
idle 0.8
shot vq_keeper_a2
idle 0.8
shot vq_keeper_a3
idle 0.8
shot vq_keeper_a4
idle 0.8
shot vq_keeper_a5
idle 0.8
shot vq_keeper_a6
idle 0.8
shot vq_keeper_a7
idle 0.8
shot vq_keeper_a8
assistwin 900
wait Credits 180
idle 3
shot vq_credits
end
