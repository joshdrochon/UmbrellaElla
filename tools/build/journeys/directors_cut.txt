# Director's Cut WIN path (title menu item 2): route start, walk CP1..CP3 to the Keeper handoff, Keeper fight to the win,
# Director's Cut story, credits, back to the Start screen. `route` uses real keys only (D held, Space hop, E dash) and is
# allowed to fail (recorded); `routeassist` then places Ella at that checkpoint through the director's own
# PlaceAtCheckpoint, named in the log. `route N T 1` holds Shoot (real J) while Ella is high, so she sinks back. `assistwin` as in win.txt. Broken Mill is in no run (D-new-mill-in-route).
firstrun
wait Title 60
idle 1
click Director's Cut              # menu item 2, by mouse
skipunless Purchase 5             # once points are earned (a run before this one), Director's Cut opens Purchase first
shot d00_purchase
texts
key Escape                        # leaves Purchase (Back) to the Start screen; the mouse cannot reach Done at 640x360 (it draws under the panel)
wait Title 5
click Director's Cut
trywait Tutorial 5
keyuntil Playing Enter 0.8 30
idle 2
shot d01_route_start
texts
route 1 8                         # real input, no altitude trim: D held, Space/E tapped, nothing else held
shot d01b_route_untrimmed         # where Ella is after 8 s of that (she rises while nothing is held)
route 1 60 1                      # real input with altitude trim (J held while she is high): walk to CP1
routeassist 1
idle 1
skipunless Purchase 2             # a refuge with unspent points opens Purchase; leave it without spending
shot d02a_refuge_purchase
key Escape
idle 1
shot d02_cp1
route 2 60 1
routeassist 2
idle 1
skipunless Purchase 2             # a refuge with unspent points opens Purchase; leave it without spending
shot d03a_refuge_purchase
key Escape
idle 1
shot d03_cp2
route 3 60 1
routeassist 3
idle 1
skipunless Purchase 2             # a refuge with unspent points opens Purchase; leave it without spending
shot d04a_refuge_purchase
key Escape
idle 1
shot d04_cp3
route 4 60 1                      # past CP3 to the Keeper handoff
routeassist 4
idle 3
shot d05_keeper_handoff
texts
assistwin 900
idle 1
shot d06_story_hold
idle 4
shot d07_story_text
texts
wait Credits 180
idle 3
shot d08_credits
texts
trywait Statistics 60             # Director's Cut Journey Complete, when statistics are on
shot d09_after_credits
texts
click Main Menu                   # Journey Complete: Try Again / Main Menu (FLOW-29)
wait Title 30
idle 2
shot d10_back_at_start
end
