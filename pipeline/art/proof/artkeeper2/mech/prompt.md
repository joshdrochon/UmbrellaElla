# Keeper mechanisms (keeper-mech-front, -middle, -rear), draft round 1
Model: flash draft, aspect 16:9, 1K. Input image: pipeline/art/proof/Keeper/owl/Keeper_R10/keyed_3.png (our in-jam Keeper, for materials and line only).
One call, one sheet, three separate objects side by side on flat green, evenly spaced, each about 55% of the canvas height:
Left (front): the Keeper's grasp-and-throw arm mechanism: a small armillary sphere with a solid rim and crossing hoops, held in a jointed brass claw-arm with a hinged elbow, the claw open and ready to grasp, a short chain link and a small glass eye at the top.
Middle (middle): a ribbed brass cage cylinder with a visible spring coil inside and a curved hinged launching arm, a short chain link and a small glass eye at the top.
Rear (rear): a toothed gear ring with a second smaller gear meshing it and a striking hammer arm, a short chain link and a small glass eye at the top.
All three are rigid fabricated metal, no feathers, no flesh. Side view, level camera.
Style: dark etched storybook engraving for a 2D game sprite: brown-black ink, sepia wash, fine cross-hatching, visible paper tooth, aged brass and iron, mid-tone bronze (not near black), warm ivory highlights, heavy dark outer contour, thin candle-cream rim light (#FFF3D6). Background: one flat pure green #00FF00, edge to edge, empty, no shadow, no text. Nothing on the objects is green.
