Four animation frames of one small magic impact burst for a 2D side-view game, painted side by side on one wide 16:9 landscape picture with a flat green
field: the gold spark that flashes where Ella's shot lands, flaring open and then scattering. Only the burst is drawn, floating alone in the green. The four bursts stand in one row on the same horizontal centre line, their centres at exactly
one eighth, three eighths, five eighths and seven eighths of the picture width, so the row is evenly spaced and every burst
has the same amount of empty green on both sides. The bursts stand far apart, each surrounded by clear green, well inside the edges of the
picture. The picture is a flat, face-on emblem, seen at the distance of a game sprite.
The largest burst spans about a fifth of the picture width.

The burst is built like the gold sparkle stars of the shot study: a sharp, angular, faceted star with four long
points on the horizontal and vertical axes and four much shorter points on the diagonals, a small bright core, and a few
fine hair-thin spark streaks around it. Every point is a straight-edged blade with real weight, wide at the base and tapering to a sharp tip, like a cut spear head, solid and chunky. The points keep
exactly these angles in every frame; only their length, the core and the spread change.

Frame 1 (impact): a tight, hard, bright core of pale cream #FFF5DC with the eight points only just starting, very short and
sharp. The whole burst spans about a twentieth of the picture width. Frame 2 (full flare): the full star, the four axis points
long and sharp, each a solid blade, the four diagonal points about a third as long, warm gold #E9C46A, with a larger cream core and a few hair-thin
gold spark streaks between the points. It spans about a tenth of the picture width. Frame 3 (break apart): the points have
separated from the centre and fly outward as eight small solid gold blades with flat green showing between them; the core is small and
a dimmer cream-gold. It spans about a fifth of the picture width. Frame 4 (fade): the centre is empty green; a loose ring of five or
six tiny pointed diamond sparks and two or three short broken gold dashes, thin and pale, spans about a fifth of the picture width,
the faintest frame of the four.

The only colours are warm gold #E9C46A, amber #F5AA28, deep bronze #94611E and pale cream #FFF5DC, plus the near-black ink
outline #171614: one warm gold family, with every hue in the effect lying between amber and cream. Each blade and the core are modelled in three clear value steps: pale cream on the upper facet, warm gold in the middle, amber then deep bronze on the lower facet, with dense etched cross-hatching in bronze on the shaded side, so every shape reads as a solid carved gem. Light comes from one source, in front and slightly above,
so upper edges are brighter and lower edges are shaded. The glow stays inside each shape: the core is the brightest point
and the light fades toward the ink outline. Outside the ink outline the picture is only the flat green.

Style: a dark painterly etched storybook illustration, ink and wash with fine etched cross-hatching and a faint paper grain
inside the shapes only. Every shape, the blades included, has a heavy, confident near-black ink outline #171614 about a tenth of the blade's width at its base (never thinner than 3 px on the largest shapes), thicker on the lower edge, like a woodcut; the hair-thin streaks and tiny sparks carry a hairline ink edge so they stay gold rather than turning
black. The background is one perfectly flat, even, uniform pure green #00FF00 filling the canvas edge to edge, with the shapes cut cleanly against it. The effect is made only of gold, amber, bronze,
cream and ink. The image contains only the four bursts on one green field, and nothing else: the green field is bare, free of text, labels, numbers, grid lines, frames and watermark.
