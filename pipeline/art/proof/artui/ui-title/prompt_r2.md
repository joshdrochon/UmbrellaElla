A wide 16:9 landscape painting, a distant panoramic view at eye level, straight-on, layered in depth from a large foreground island to far misty hills.

Style: dark storybook ink and wash on aged sepia paper, soft wet washes, fine etched cross-hatching, mottled stains, heavy visible paper grain and speckled ink stipple over every wash, hand-inked contour lines on all rock, roofs and stonework, a painted vignette. Palette: soot black #171614, charcoal, umber and smoky taupe; light only as warm ivory #FFF3D6 and thin gold #E9C46A with amber #FFC76A glints; wind in muted desaturated ash blue #555E60. Grass is dry ochre-brown, flowers cream, every other tone muted brown-grey. The only lettering anywhere is the Roman numerals on the clocks; there are no people.

The left 40% of the frame, top to bottom, is an empty field of deep soot-black and charcoal mist with a few tiny gold sparks, kept open for lettering, holding no shapes, clock or frame. It fades into smoky taupe fog near the 40% line.

The right 60% holds the scene. A large rocky island fills the lower right and runs off the bottom and right edges, its top and every broken rock edge rimmed with thin, bright gold light like gilded cracks, its underside a ragged mass of dripping stalactites. On it stand a timber-framed cottage with a steep dark slate roof, two chimneys, ivy, a thread of white smoke and two glowing amber windows; a small lantern on a post; a wooden fence; and a wooden windmill beside it with four torn cream canvas sails. At centre-left a mid-sized island holds a tall ruined stone arch; below it a smaller island carries a low stone bridge arch. A few tiny islands drift between, and a dark island fragment is cropped at the top-right corner. Behind them, rolling misty ridges recede in layers; on the far ridges sit a small spired castle silhouette and rows of broken aqueduct arches. In the upper-right sky hang a huge ghostly clock face and a smaller one overlapping it, drawn in thin etched ink line with Roman numerals and one long pointed hand tipped in gold, half hidden by cloud, with a warm gold-ochre glow behind them, the brightest part of the picture. Long, thin, translucent ribbons of smoky ash-blue wind sweep in S-curves from the lower left up to the upper right.
