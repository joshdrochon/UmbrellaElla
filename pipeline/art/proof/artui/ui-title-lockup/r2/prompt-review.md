Edits (exact replacements):
1. Para 3, replace "brushed by hand with a dry brush, not set from a font" with "cut like engraved letterpress display capitals with sharp flared wedge serifs and a slight storybook flair, stamped by hand so each letter differs a little". Why: the reference reads as worn gold-foil stamping on an old book cover, not brush script; "dry brush" risks a brush-script face and does not fix r19's "no etched feel".
2. Para 1, replace "with nothing else on it: no texture, no glow, no border" with "and the black stays empty and edge-to-edge clean around crisp, unlit letter edges".
3. Para 7, replace "small worn Roman numerals XI, XII and I sit on the outside of the arc, above the letters, never on top of them" with "small worn Roman numerals XI, XII and I sit just inside the arc, between the arc and the tops of the letters, in clear black space, with fine tick marks along the arc and a short double rim where it ends". Why: the reference puts numerals inside the ring and shows ticks and a double rim; the prompt leaves both out.
4. Para 7, replace "in dim sepia-brown #6B5434" with "in faded, worn bronze-gold #8A6A3A, broken and rubbed away in places". Why: the reference arc is a light worn gold that reads on black; #6B5434 is close to black and will key out or vanish.
5. Para 7, replace "rises from behind the first U, curves over the top of the words and comes down behind the last A" with "is the upper-right part of a large clock face: it starts above the M, curves over the top of the words and comes down past the final A to meet the clock hand's tip". Why: the reference arc is asymmetric and ties into the hand.
6. Para 9, replace "a thin curling ink tail trails from the pivot back under the second line. The hand stays in the clear black space to the right and below; it never crosses a letter." with "a short curling ink tail curls from the pivot back toward the last letter, staying level with the second line's baseline. The hand and tail live entirely in the clear black space to the right of both lines, above the second line's baseline." Why: "below" and "under the second line" break the constraint that nothing overhangs the menu.
7. Para 11, replace "with no other words or letters" with "and these two lines are the only lettering in the image".
