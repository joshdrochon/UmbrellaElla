1. Open with the medium and the pose, not "Paint". Replace the first sentence with: "An etched ink engraving of the Keeper of Time, a large crownless mechanical owl of old metal, hovering in pure side view facing left with its wings half raised in a calm mid-beat, mysterious and quietly menacing." Then move the Pose paragraph directly after it, before Camera and framing.
2. Drop "painted" (it pulls a painterly finish against STYLE_BLOCK's engraving). Replace `matching a dark painted "other realm" night study:` with `a dark night palette:`.
3. STYLE_BLOCK conflict (real): it says "aged paper grain ... under all colour" and "No flat fills"; the background asks for one flat fill. Replace the last sentence `Any paper grain stays inside the owl only.` with: "The paper grain, hatching and ink texture live only inside the owl's cream outline; the green background is the one flat fill in the image, untouched by paper or ink." Also ask the builder to confirm assetgen puts STYLE_BLOCK after this paragraph (it does: last word goes to "No flat fills"), so this sentence must be explicit.
4. Negatives to positives (Gemini primes on named nouns, worst for "crown"):
   - `no feathers, no fur, no flesh, no plumage` -> "every surface is metal: plates, vanes, rivets, hinges and glass".
   - `no crown, no crest` -> "the top of the head is a low smooth dome of plates between the two ear plates".
   - `No bright gold, no shiny brass, no saturated colour` -> "all metal is dull and tarnished, matte brown and grey".
   - `No green anywhere on the owl` -> "the metal is warm brown and grey, with no verdigris or green patina" (bronze invites verdigris; it would key out).
5. Proportions missing. Add after the torso sentence: "The clock face fills most of the chest, about half the torso's height; the head is small, about a third of the torso's height, set forward and above the clock; the wings are the largest mass, together about twice the body's length."
6. Wing shape (from the crop). Add: "Each wing's leading edge rises from the shoulder up and back; the long pointed vanes sweep to tips at the upper right, layered in overlapping rows."
7. Legs: crop shows them dangling, not tucked. Replace `tucked under the body` with "hanging straight down below the clock, plated shins, three forward talons curled".
8. Tail: add "the tail plates angle down and back toward the lower right".
9. Video and keying. Add a sentence: "Clear green gaps separate each chain from the legs, the tail and the other chains, and the far wing from the near wing, so each part can move on its own; every edge is crisp, with no motion blur, smoke, glow or soft halo."
10. Outline: "2 to 3 pixels" means little to the model. Replace with "a thin, even pale cream outline, like the in-jam Ella sprite's".
11. Keep the hex codes only once (they are fine) but say "colours close to" so they are not lettered into the image; framing numbers (60%/70%) are fine and the request's --aspect must be 16:9.
