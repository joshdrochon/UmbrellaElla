**Corrections**
- **Colour:** the prompt says "pale cream #D8C8A8 to umber #8A7358 with a burn along the borders". The reference is a mid taupe with almost no value range. Drop the cream, the umber and the burn. Ask for one even mid taupe-brown with soft cloudy drift.
- **Grain:** the prompt's "fibres, flecks, water stains with dark rims, creases, folds, foxing" is too busy and too aged. The reference grain is a fine woven linen or canvas weave, plus soft mottling.
- **Wind lines:** the prompt asks for single lines with varying weight, mostly horizontal. The reference has bundles of 5–10 parallel hairlines that sway as one ribbon. They sit in the upper and lower bands, with the centre clear.
- **Line colour and density:** in the tile the lines are pale, light-embossed lines. In the key art they are dark brown. Decide which one this piece is. "One tenth of the area" is a number the model can't use. Describe the placement in words instead.
- **Sparkles:** "four-point sparkle rings" is wrong. The reference has round soft dots, some with a thin ring around them, and no star points. Say "about six".
- **Gemini best practice:**
  - The closing sentence is a list of negatives ("no characters, buildings, horizon, frame, border, lettering, numbers or watermark"). It names the things you don't want, which primes them. Describe the empty state instead.
  - "Dark gothic engraving book endpaper" and "storybook" invite book, page and frame imagery.
  - The opening line does state the camera, the view and the aspect, which is good. Add the ratio and size (3200x1080, so about 3:1) to the call settings.
  - "Stays within sepia, taupe, tan and umber" repeats a keyword list.
  - Keep the single fixed style block.
