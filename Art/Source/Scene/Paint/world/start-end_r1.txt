A wide 16:9, edge-to-edge, text-free, character-free landscape of a dark other-worldly sky realm, seen side-on from a level camera at a great distance, as a dark storybook illustration. No border, frame, lettering or watermark.

The paper itself is near-black charcoal; its grain shows only as faint lighter fibre, so the sky stays almost black. The sky is a warm charcoal void (#1a1714 to #24201b), slightly lighter and hazier through the middle height and darkest at the top and bottom corners. Scattered thinly across it, a hundred or so tiny ember flecks of dim amber float like distant sparks and read as small gold lights.

Far away and very low in contrast, ghostly giant clock rims hang in the sky: huge thin bronze-grey clock rims, seen only as great partial arcs, sagging and melting so their lower edges drip downward in long thin wax-like tendrils, with faint hour tick marks along each arc. They are barely lighter than the sky (#3a332b). Two or three overlap at different depths: one in the upper left, one across the middle right, one low in the lower left. Long, soft, rolling haze ridges in dark umber drift in horizontal layers between them like distant smoky dunes, each a little lighter at its top edge. A very faint ash-blue cast lies in the haze in a few places.

Kept below the middle of the frame, in the lower right third, floats one substantial eroded island of dark bronze-brown earth: a flat, slightly uneven crust on top with a thin warm gold rim light along its upper edge (#a8784a), and an underside hanging in long, swept, wax-like drips and tendrils, with a spray of dark specks beneath, as if the land were melting into the void. On it stand the dark ruin of a small wooden barn, its broken roof sagging and trailing in thin webbed shreds, and beside it a skeletal windmill: a thin ring wheel with a few curved web-like spokes, its sails curling like dry branches. A second, much smaller eroded island floats at the left edge just below middle height, cut off by the frame, with the same melting underside.

Lighting is subdued and comes softly from the upper back, so every form is mostly in deep shadow with only a thin warm edge of light. The centre and the whole upper half stay quiet, dark and empty. Colours are given only as guidance; never print them.
