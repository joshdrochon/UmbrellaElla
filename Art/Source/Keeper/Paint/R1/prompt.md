## Call
The Keeper of Time, a large fabricated mechanical owl, in pure side view facing left, hovering and flapping slowly. One 21:9 strip of 4 poses in a row, level camera, same scale, each owl whole and about 70% of the canvas height, evenly spaced with wide empty green gaps. Pose 1: both wings raised high above the back, tips pointing up. Pose 2: wings half way down, held level and spread wide. Pose 3: wings swept low below the body, tips pointing down. Pose 4: wings half way up, level and spread. Body, head, clock, legs and the three hanging mechanisms stay the same size and place in all four; only the wings move.

Identity: a compact round torso centred on an aged clock face with Roman numerals, glass and one long hand. A small angular owl head with a heavy brow, a short hooked beak and one dull glass eye; no crown, no feathers, no flesh. Two very broad wings of rigid riveted metal vane plates, ridged and perforated, joined to the body by bevelled spars and hinges. Two short mechanical legs ending in exactly two clawed feet. A plated tail. Below the body hang exactly three separate mechanisms on their own chains: in front a small armillary sphere with a solid rim and crossing hoops; in the middle a ribbed cage cylinder; at the rear a toothed gear ring. The attached image shows this owl in a different wing pose; copy its design and materials, not its wings.

Style: dark etched storybook engraving for a 2D game sprite: brown-black ink, sepia wash, fine cross-hatching and visible paper tooth. Quiet iron, steel and dull bronze, warm ivory highlights, a heavy dark outer contour. Dark-on-dark interior detail. A thin rim light in warm candle-cream (#FFF3D6, more red than green, never lemon or lime). Only the clock face glows a soft gold (#E9C46A). Feet hang below the body, off any ground. Background: one flat pure green #00FF00, edge to edge, unbroken, empty. Nothing on the owl is green. No text, no shadow on the ground.
